How it works
Brief in, shortlist out — one pass

Eleven automated stages, from a plain-English brief to recruiter-approved outreach.

1

Understand the brief

An LLM parses your plain-English brief into structured criteria — explicit fields always override the model.

2

Search every channel

Boolean X-ray queries per platform return people, not job ads — across six sources plus the open web.

3

De-dupe & rank

Entity resolution merges duplicates; an LLM scores each candidate 0–10 against the brief with a hard experience floor.

4

Enrich & verify

Verified email, title and company where available; a discovered CV is checked against the candidate's name before it's trusted.

5

Confidence-tier

Every candidate gets a High / Medium / Low label with reasons, so you know exactly which rows to trust.

6

Approve & reach out

You tick "Send Email?" on chosen rows; personalized outreach sends and replies auto-flag. Nothing goes out without you.

Built for responsible hiring
Trust, by design

This pipeline handles real people's data, so it keeps a human in control and minimizes what it collects.

Recruiter-in-the-loop

The system ranks and enriches but never contacts anyone on its own — outreach only sends after you explicitly approve a row.

Credentials stay in n8n

API keys live in n8n's credential store, never in the repo or the exported template, which ships with placeholders only.

Data minimization

Only public professional data is used; enrichment is credit-aware and skipped where it isn't needed.

Bias & compliance aware

Keep criteria job-related and treat scores as one signal. Configure retention and opt-outs to fit GDPR / CCPA and local law.
