How it works — one pass

  1. IntakeRecruiter submits role, skills, location, seniority, exclusions, target platforms — or pastes/uploads a full JD.
  2. Natural-language understandingAn LLM parses the free-text brief into structured criteria; explicit form fields always override the AI.
  3. Query constructionBoolean "X-ray" strings per platform with synonym expansion, exclusions, and a job-post noise filter.
  4. Multi-source searchPaginated search via Serper across selected platforms and the open web; results merged and de-duplicated.
  5. AI rankingEvery result scored 0–10 against the criteria, with a hard experience-minimum and rules to reject non-person pages.
  6. EnrichmentTop candidates enriched with verified work email/title/company (Apollo) and public GitHub data.
  7. Resume discoveryA targeted secondary search finds a downloadable CV — verified against the candidate's name before it's trusted.
  8. Confidence scoringEach candidate gets a High/Medium/Low label with reasons, so recruiters know which rows to trust.
  9. CRM writeDeduped candidates upserted into a Google Sheets tracker (25+ fields), plus per-candidate cards to Slack.
  10. Approved outreachRecruiter ticks "Send Email?"; a scheduler sends personalized outreach and auto-flags replies.
  11. GovernanceMonthly API-credit budget tracking with auto-reset and an 80% near-cap alert.

Key features

Boolean X-ray automation

Google/Bing X-ray queries built per platform to return people, not job ads.

LLM ranking & scoring

Every match scored against the brief, with hard filters and non-person rejection.

Data enrichment

Verified email, title, company, and public profile data — credit-aware to save spend.

Confidence & review flow

Transparent High/Med/Low labels with reasons for a human-in-the-loop review.

Export & ATS-ready

Deduped rows written to a tracker with 25+ fields; export to CSV/ATS.

Approved outreach

Personalized emails sent only on recruiter approval, with reply auto-flagging.

Emphasis on data quality, judgment, and respecting the limits of automation — a working proof that recruiters can build, not just operate an ATS.

Responsible use & data privacy

This pipeline handles personal data about real candidates, so it is built to keep a human in control and to minimize what it collects and retains.

  • Recruiter-in-the-loop by design. The system ranks and enriches, but never contacts anyone on its own — outreach only sends after a recruiter explicitly ticks "Send Email?" on a row. AI scores are decision support, not decisions.
  • Credentials stay in n8n. API keys and tokens live in n8n's credential store, never in the repo or the exported template (which ships with YOUR_* placeholders).
  • Data minimization. Only publicly available professional data is used; enrichment is credit-aware and skipped where it isn't needed; and confidence labels let recruiters set aside low-trust rows rather than act on them.
  • Bias & fairness. Boolean and AI matching can inherit bias from queries and sources. Keep criteria job-related, review the shortlist yourself, and treat scores as one signal among many — this widens and speeds sourcing, it does not auto-reject people.
  • Compliance is your responsibility. Depending on jurisdiction, sourcing and outreach may fall under GDPR, CCPA, or local employment law — lawful basis, data-subject requests, retention limits, and outreach consent. Configure retention, opt-outs, and record-keeping to fit your obligations.
