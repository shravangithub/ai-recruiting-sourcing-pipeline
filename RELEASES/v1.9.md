# v1.9: Write to it like a colleague

_AI Recruiting Sourcing Pipeline · built on [n8n](https://n8n.io)_

Recruiters can now email the sourcing mailbox, or message the Telegram bot, the way they would brief
a sourcer: any subject, any wording, and replies in the same thread to change the search or ask
about the people. Scores now separate people instead of giving the top 25 all 10/10.

## The sourcing assistant

Every email from a teammate (and every Telegram message that isn't a command) goes to one assistant
that reads it with the conversation so far, the team's roles and the people on the role it's about,
then does one thing:

- **Search**: "need 5 senior PMs in Bangalore, B2B SaaS, 6-10 yrs". Channels named in the message are
  the channels searched; "everywhere" means all of them.
- **Change a search**, in a reply: "make it 8+ years and drop Reddit", "more like #1".
- **Show people**: "top 5", "only the ones with an email", "who's shortlisted".
- **Explain or compare**: "why is #2 above #3", "compare 1 and 2".
- **Add a person**: a forwarded profile link or CV, "pls check for the java role".
- **Draft a LinkedIn note** for someone without an email, for the recruiter to send.
- **Status**, **help**, or **one question back** when it can't tell what was meant.

Names, scores and links in a reply come from the Board. It never says it did something it didn't,
it declines what it can't do from email or Telegram (stopping a search, emailing a candidate), and
a search needs a role in the message, so "can you find people?" asks which role instead of running
an old search again. Results emails and replies carry the search they're about, so a reply in that
thread is read against it.

The `Search: <role>` and `Fwd: <role>` formats still work.

## Scores that tell people apart

The AI now rates each person on each requirement of the role (the recruiter's rubric, or the role,
each must-have, years, city and nice-to-haves): shown, partly, not shown, or contradicted, quoting
the words from their profile. The score is the weighted share of points, worked out in code, and the
summary ends with a short "Scored:" line, e.g.
`Scored: Role ✓ ("QA Automation Engineer"); Selenium ✓; Java ~; Experience 4-7 years ?; Based in Chennai ✓.`
People added by link, email or Telegram are rated the same way.

## Fixes

- Email-in reads every inbox message, read or unread, every minute, and never reads the pipeline's own
  mail (results, replies, weekly report). A teammate's reply that quotes ours is read.
- A Google page that fails is retried and no longer stops the search; searches still "running" after
  30 minutes are marked Stopped, with an email saying which.
- Adding a person no longer sends the weekly report, and the Health and weekly-preview pages work again.
- An experience range written in a brief ("6-10 years") is kept as written.

## Under the hood

- New app route `assist`: **Assist Prompt** → **Assist Planner** (gpt-5-mini) → **Assist Act** → the
  app's own routes → **Assist Write Prompt** → **Assist Writer** (gpt-5-mini) → **Assist Reply**.
  `dry: true` plans and answers without starting anything.
- Email: **Email Text** → **Email Thread** (the Gmail thread) → **Email Ask** → the app.
- Scoring: **Format Search Results** builds numbered criteria; **Split Candidates** and **Add Row**
  compute the score from the ratings.
- About two gpt-5-mini calls per email or message; searches cost the same as before.
