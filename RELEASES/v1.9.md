# v1.9: Write to it like a colleague

_AI Recruiting Sourcing Pipeline · built on [n8n](https://n8n.io)_

Recruiters can now email the sourcing mailbox, or message the Telegram bot, the way they would brief
a sourcer: any subject, any wording, and replies in the same thread to change the search or ask
about the people. Scores now separate people instead of giving the top 25 all 10/10.

## The sourcing assistant

Every email from a teammate (and every Telegram message that isn't a command) goes to one assistant
that reads it with the conversation so far, the team's roles and the people on the role it's about,
then does one thing:

- **Search**: "need 5 senior PMs in Bangalore, B2B SaaS, 6-10 yrs". Channels named in the message are
  the channels searched; "everywhere" means all of them.
- **Change a search**, in a reply: "make it 8+ years and drop Reddit", "more like #1".
- **Show people**: "top 5", "only the ones with an email", "who's shortlisted".
- **Explain or compare**: "why is #2 above #3", "compare 1 and 2".
- **Add a person**: a forwarded profile link or CV, "pls check for the java role".
- **Draft a LinkedIn note** for someone without an email, for the recruiter to send.
- **Status**, **help**, or **one question back** when it can't tell what was meant.

Names, scores and links in a reply come from the Board. It never says it did something it didn't,
it declines what it can't do from email or Telegram (stopping a search, emailing a candidate), and
a search needs a role in the message, so "can you find people?" asks which role instead of running
an old search again. Results emails and replies carry the search they're about, so a reply in that
thread is read against it.

The `Search: <role>` and `Fwd: <role>` formats still work.

## Scores that tell people apart

The AI now rates each person on each requirement of the role (the recruiter's rubric, or the role,
each must-have, years, city and nice-to-haves): shown, partly, not shown, or contradicted, quoting
the words from their profile. The score is the weighted share of points, worked out in code, and the
summary ends with a short "Scored:" line, e.g.
`Scored: Role ✓ ("QA Automation Engineer"); Selenium ✓; Java ~; Experience 4-7 years ?; Based in Chennai ✓.`
People added by link, email or Telegram are rated the same way.

## Email lookups only for people you pick

Searches no longer spend Apollo credits on everyone in the top 25. When you move someone to
**Shortlisted** (on the Board, or by asking the assistant by email or Telegram), or approve someone
with no email, their email is looked up then: Apollo first, then [Hunter](https://hunter.io) when
Apollo has none, is out of credits or isn't set up. A found email shows on the card straight away and
is saved in the results sheet, so Approve works. When neither finds one, the Board says why and
suggests a LinkedIn note. Hunter is optional: add a `Sourcing · Hunter` connection (Header Auth,
name `X-API-KEY`) or a Hunter key in setup.

## Shortlist from email or Telegram

"shortlist 1 and 3", "#5 is not a fit", "move #2 to interview": the assistant moves the people on the
Board and, for a shortlist, looks up their emails and says which it found. Approving (which emails
the person) stays on the Board.

## More people per search

- Apollo's people search uses no credits, so it now brings back up to 200 people (2 pages of 100) with
  similar titles ("Senior DevOps Engineer" also finds "DevOps Engineer" and close titles), and runs even
  when the month's Apollo credits are used.
- LinkedIn gets two more searches: the role without the must-have skills (profiles often don't list
  every skill; the AI scores them) and the role's other common titles ("Platform Engineer" for DevOps).
- The resume look-up runs for the top 8 instead of the top 20, which pays for the extra searches.
- The results email says how many profiles were reviewed.

## A review list for big searches

When a search finds more than 30 people, the Board opens on **Review list**: everyone found, split into
Strong (85%+), Good (70–84%) and Maybe, each with a progress bar of how many you've reviewed. The list on the
left is two lines per person; the panel on the right shows the profile, why they scored what they did
(each requirement shown, partly shown or not shown, with the words from their profile) and Shortlist / Not
a fit / Skip. Keys: J/K to move, S shortlist, X not a fit, N skip, Enter for the full profile. Search and
source filters narrow the list. **Pipeline** switches back to the stage columns.

## Exa people search

A new channel that runs with LinkedIn: Exa searches over a billion public profiles from the brief, in plain
English, and returns 100 full profiles (headline, company, city, about) for about $0.10. They are scored with
everyone else and show as "LinkedIn (Exa)". In a test of 1,350 Indian profiles across six stacks, three
experience bands and three cities, about 88% matched the city, stack and years asked for (Product Manager
and 8+ years were weakest, around 80%). The other channels (Google, GitHub, Stack Overflow, Kaggle, Hacker
News, Reddit and the rest) are unchanged. Exa is optional: add a `Sourcing · Exa` connection (Header Auth,
name `x-api-key`) or an Exa key in setup.

## How sure an email is

Every email says where it came from and how sure it is: "Apollo · verified", "Apollo · guessed,
not verified" (built from the company's email format) or "Hunter · likely (82%), not verified".
Unverified ones show in amber on the Board and the assistant says they may bounce.

## Credits kept for people you pick

A search enriches at most the top 15 people from Apollo's own search; everyone else is looked up when
shortlisted. When Apollo or Hunter has no email for someone, that answer is remembered and they are not
asked again for 30 days. "Out of credits" is not remembered, so **Find emails** on the Shortlisted
column tries again after a top-up (up to 10 people, one credit each).

## What each search cost

The Board and the results email show what a search used: Google searches (Serper), AI tokens
(OpenAI) and any Apollo credits, with a rough dollar figure at list prices.

## Test mode

Try the whole pipeline with sample data without any risk of emailing a real candidate.

- **Settings → Sending → Test mode.** When on, every outreach email goes to a **test
  address** you set (subject tagged `[TEST]`), never to the candidate. A banner on the board
  shows it's on and where mail is going.
- Test contacts start a sequence marked **"test"** that is never followed up, and **follow-ups
  pause for everyone** while test mode is on — so no real person can be emailed, even one already
  mid-sequence.
- Daily send limits and the unverified-email hold are relaxed in test mode, so your sample rows
  all flow through to the test inbox.
- Turn it off to run for real.

## Privacy and safe sending

Built for India's DPDP Act and for keeping a new mailbox out of spam.

- **Every email says why.** The first email ends with a short notice: where the person's public
  profile was found, for which role, what is kept and why. Follow-ups carry a shorter one.
- **Stop or delete, from the email.** Each email links to a page where the person can stop the
  emails, or stop them and have their details deleted. The page asks first, so mail scanners that
  open links can't delete anyone. The link needs your n8n's public address (Settings → Sending);
  without it, emails ask people to reply "remove me".
- **Delete from the Board.** Open a person → *If they ask* → **Stop emails** or **Delete their
  details** (press twice). For a "remove me" reply, a phone call or any other request.
- **Deleting means everywhere, for every role:** the board, stages, history, email sequences and
  the sheet row (redacted). Only a note that the address asked not to be contacted is kept.
- **Opted-out people stay out.** They are never emailed again and later searches don't add them back.
- **Retention.** Every night, people nobody shortlisted, approved or emailed are deleted after
  12 months (Settings → Sending; 0 keeps them).
- **A daily limit on new people emailed** (default 30) on top of the follow-up limit.
- **Unverified emails wait.** Guessed or unverified addresses are held back from the auto-sender
  until an admin switches on *Email unverified addresses*.

## Fixes

- Email-in reads every inbox message, read or unread, every minute, and never reads the pipeline's own
  mail (results, replies, weekly report). A teammate's reply that quotes ours is read.
- A Google page that fails is retried and no longer stops the search; searches still "running" after
  30 minutes are marked Stopped, with an email saying which.
- Adding a person no longer sends the weekly report, and the Health and weekly-preview pages work again.
- An experience range written in a brief ("6-10 years") is kept as written.

## Under the hood

- Opt-outs: data table `app_optout` (token, email, contact_key, status, dates). Webhooks
  `GET /webhook/sourcing-optout` (the choice page) and `POST /webhook/sourcing-optout-do`; app route
  `forget`. Both run **Opt-out Who** → **Opt-out Plan** → one write per table → **Opt-out Finish**.
- Retention: **Retention Schedule** (02:41 daily) → **Retention Plan** → deletes, at most 400 a night.
- If the opt-out list can't be read, the outreach and follow-up senders send nothing that round.

- New app route `assist`: **Assist Prompt** → **Assist Planner** (gpt-5-mini) → **Assist Act** → the
  app's own routes → **Assist Write Prompt** → **Assist Writer** (gpt-5-mini) → **Assist Reply**.
  `dry: true` plans and answers without starting anything.
- Email: **Email Text** → **Email Thread** (the Gmail thread) → **Email Ask** → the app.
- Scoring: **Format Search Results** builds numbered criteria; **Split Candidates** and **Add Row**
  compute the score from the ratings.
- Email lookup: `update_card` to Shortlisted/Approved, or `find_email`, → **Lookup Person** → **Lookup Prep**
  → **Apollo Lookup** → **Apollo Says** → **Hunter Lookup** → **Lookup Result** → **Save Found Email** and
  **Found Email In Sheet**.
- About two gpt-5-mini calls per email or message; searches use fewer Apollo credits than before
  (one per shortlisted person without an email).
