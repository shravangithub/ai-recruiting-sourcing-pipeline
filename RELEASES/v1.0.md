# v1.0 — Initial release

_AI Recruiting Sourcing Pipeline · built on [n8n](https://n8n.io)_

The first public release of an evidence-based, AI-powered candidate sourcing and outreach
pipeline. It turns a plain-English hiring brief (or an uploaded job description) into a
ranked, enriched, confidence-scored shortlist of real candidates — and manages
recruiter-approved outreach end to end.

## Highlights

- **Brief → structured criteria.** An LLM parses a free-text brief or JD into structured search
  criteria; explicit form fields always override the AI.
- **Boolean X-ray search.** Per-platform query construction with synonym expansion, exclusions,
  education filters, and a job-post noise filter so results are *people*, not job ads.
- **Multi-source, de-duplicated search.** Paginated search across selected platforms and the open
  web, merged and de-duplicated.
- **AI ranking (0–10).** Every result scored against the criteria, with a hard experience-minimum
  and rules to reject postings, directories, and non-person pages.
- **Enrichment.** Verified work email / title / company and public GitHub data for top candidates,
  with credit-aware skipping to control spend.
- **Resume discovery.** A targeted secondary search finds a downloadable CV, verified against the
  candidate's name before it's trusted.
- **Confidence scoring.** High / Medium / Low labels with reasons, so recruiters know which rows to
  trust.
- **CRM write + Slack cards.** Deduped candidates upserted into a Google Sheets tracker (25+ fields),
  with per-candidate cards posted to Slack.
- **Approved outreach.** Recruiter-gated, personalized email sending with automatic reply flagging.
- **Governance.** Monthly API-credit budget tracking with auto-reset and an 80% near-cap alert.

## Getting it

- Import the n8n template: [`ai-recruiting-pipeline.template.json`](../ai-recruiting-pipeline.template.json)
- Demo & docs: [live site](https://shravangithub.github.io/ai-recruiting-sourcing-pipeline/)

## Known limitations

- Enrichment and search quality depend on your provider credentials (LLM, Serper, Apollo).
- Rankings are assistive, not a substitute for recruiter judgment — the confidence labels exist
  precisely so a human reviews the borderline rows.
