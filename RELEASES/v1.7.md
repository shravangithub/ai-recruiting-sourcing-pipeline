# v1.7: Source from your phone, on Telegram

_AI Recruiting Sourcing Pipeline · built on [n8n](https://n8n.io)_

A Telegram bot for the team. It works through the same API as the app, as the teammate who is
texting, so everything shows up on the Board. No public address needed: n8n asks Telegram for new
messages every 30 seconds.

## Set-up (five minutes)

1. In Telegram, open **BotFather**, send `/newbot`, follow the two questions, and copy the token.
2. In the app, **Settings → Telegram**, paste the token (admins only) and save.
3. Each teammate presses **Connect Telegram**, gets a one-time code, and sends `/start CODE` to the bot.
   Only connected teammates can use it.

## What you can text

| You send | What happens |
|---|---|
| `Product manager in Bangalore, 5-8 years, B2B SaaS` | Reads the brief, confirms what it understood and starts a search. The top 5 arrive when it's done. |
| `roles` | Your open roles, numbered. `role 2` switches to one. |
| `top`, `more` | The best people on the current role, 5 at a time, numbered, with why they fit and a link. |
| `shortlist 1 3`, `not fit 2`, `approve 1` | Acts on the numbered list. The Board updates. |
| `status` | Whether searches, follow-ups and sending are running, with fixes. |
| `help` | The list above. |

When a candidate replies, everyone connected gets the reply with a drafted answer and free calendar
slots: `send R1` sends the draft from Gmail, `send R1: your own words` sends yours, `later R1` parks
them for 90 days.

## Under the hood

- Messages, lists and drafts are plain text; nothing is sent to a candidate without a `send`.
- The bot token lives in `app_settings` inside your n8n. A service key, also inside n8n, lets the bot
  act as a connected teammate; it never leaves the machine.
- New steps: **Telegram Poll** (every 30 s) → **TG Jobs** → two calls to the app's own API → **TG Compose**
  → **Telegram Send**. If n8n runs on another port, set `self_url` in `app_settings`.
