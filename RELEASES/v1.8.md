# v1.8: Where recruiters already are

_AI Recruiting Sourcing Pipeline · built on [n8n](https://n8n.io)_

Four more ways to put a person on the Board without opening the app: a button in the browser, a
forwarded email, a voice note on Telegram, and the app itself on a phone's home screen. All of them
score the person against the role's brief with the same prompt as a search, so Found stays
comparable whoever added whom.

## Add to Board, from any page

In **Settings → Phone & browser**, drag the **Add to Board** button to your bookmarks bar. On a
LinkedIn, GitHub or any profile page, click it: a small window asks which role (or picks it when
you passed one), reads the page, and shows the score, the summary and whether an email address was
found. The person lands in Found as "Added from the browser by <you>". Clicking it twice on the
same page says "Already there".

The button carries a personal key, not your password; it can only add people, and **Make a new
button** retires the old one.

## By email

Forward a CV or a profile link to the mailbox connected in n8n, with the role name in the subject
(`Fwd: Backend Engineer, Pune`). Only teammates in the workspace are listened to; everyone else's
mail is left alone. A PDF attached to the mail is read as the CV. You get a reply with the score,
or a list of your roles if the subject didn't name one.

## Voice notes on Telegram

Hold the mic and describe the role; the bot transcribes it (OpenAI, same key as scoring) and
treats it as if you'd typed it, so a search starts. Sending a profile link on its own adds that
person to the role you're on.

## On your phone

Sign in with **Stay signed in on this device** ticked and the session lasts 30 days instead of 7,
kept in the page address, which is the one place n8n's sandboxed pages are allowed to keep anything.
Add the page to the home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and it opens like an app, signed in,
with its own icon. Sign out clears it.

## Channels in the brief, and search by email

Name the channels in a Telegram brief (typed or spoken) and those are the ones searched: "…B2B SaaS,
on LinkedIn, GitHub and research papers". "Everywhere" picks all 25. Say nothing and the defaults
apply. "Ex-servicemen" or "veterans" turns the veteran search on. The bot's "Got it" reply names
the channels.

Email with the subject `Search: Product Manager, Bangalore` and the brief in the body starts a
search the same way; you get a reply saying what's being searched, and the usual results email
when it's done. The full form (rubric, exclusions, similar profiles) stays in the app.

## Under the hood

- One route, `add_person`, serves all four; **Add Prompt** picks the role (by id, or by the words in
  a subject), **Add Agent** scores, **Add Row** writes the card with the source named.
- Email in branches off **On Candidate Reply** (the existing Gmail poll): **Email In** keeps only
  active teammates' mail, **Email Get** fetches attachments, **Extract Email PDF** reads a CV,
  **Email Add** calls the app's API with the service key, **Email Answer** replies.
- Voice: **TG Has Voice?** → **TG Voices** → Telegram `getFile` → download → **Transcribe**
  (`gpt-4o-mini-transcribe`) → **TG Transcripts**, read by **TG Jobs**.
- **App Icon** (`/webhook/sourcing-app-icon`) serves the logo for the home screen.
- Search by email: **Email Search?** → **Email Search Body** (channels from the brief, results to the sender) → **Email Start Search**.
- 339 steps; 154 tests.
