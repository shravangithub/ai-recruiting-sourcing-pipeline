# v1.1 — More channels, non-tech roles, a smarter app

_AI Recruiting Sourcing Pipeline · built on [n8n](https://n8n.io)_

## New sources (23 in total)

| Group | Sources | How |
|---|---|---|
| Hackathons | Devfolio, Unstop, Devpost, HackerEarth | One Google search per site, aimed at profile pages, by skills and city |
| Competitive programming | LeetCode, CodeChef, Codeforces | Profile pages by city. Codeforces profiles get name, city, organisation and rating from Codeforces' free public API |
| Product and non-tech | Topmate, ADPList, Medium | Mentor and profile pages by job title and city |

Only people's profile pages are kept: contest pages, problems, projects, posts and category lists are
dropped. Tested live: a "Software Engineer, Bangalore, 0-2 years" search across the hackathon and contest
sites found 47 people. A "Product Manager, Bangalore" search across Topmate, ADPList and Medium found 24.

Tried and left out: Product Hunt and Substack (Google shows their posts, not people), Wellfound
(profiles aren't indexed), and Naukri, iimjobs, Instahyre and Indeed (private, paid recruiter databases).
Unstop and CodeChef are available, but Google indexes few of their profiles.

## Non-tech roles

- **Title variants** for product, program, sales, marketing, HR, finance, operations and customer-success
  roles. "HR Business Partner" also finds "HRBP" and "People Partner". "Area Sales Manager" also finds
  "Sales Manager" and "Territory Sales Manager".
- **Colleges filter** (optional, up to 3) next to target companies, used in the LinkedIn search.
  When the brief only *prefers* a college ("ideally IIM"), the college counts towards the ranking and
  doesn't filter the search.
- **Scoring on scope and outcomes**: for non-tech roles the AI weighs team size, targets, budgets,
  measurable results, seniority and domain, not code. Generic duties like "team management" are no
  longer used as search words.
- The intake agent suggests sources by role: PMs → LinkedIn, Topmate, ADPList; sales, marketing, HR,
  finance and ops → LinkedIn, Topmate, Open Web and Medium.

## The app

- **Why they match**: open a candidate to see each must-have with the sentence from their profile
  that shows it, or a note to ask about it on the first call. The panel also shows experience and
  location checks, where they were found, their contact details and the follow-up plan.
- **Votes re-rank**: a thumbs-up moves that person to the top and lifts people with the same skills,
  company or source ("▲ like your picks"). A thumbs-down sinks them and their lookalikes.
- **Edit follow-up emails** in Outreach: the day 2, 4, 5 and 7 notes, with placeholders and a live
  preview. Text is saved as simple paragraphs; anything that looks like HTML is shown as text.

## Fixes

- A search that finds nobody now finishes with "no matching profiles found". Before, it failed at the
  scoring step and the app showed it as running.
