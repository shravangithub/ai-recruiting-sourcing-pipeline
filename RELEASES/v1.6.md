# v1.6: A public apply page for every role

_AI Recruiting Sourcing Pipeline · built on [n8n](https://n8n.io)_

- **Apply page per role.** On the Board, switch on "Apply page" for a role and copy its link. Post it on
  LinkedIn, in communities or in a job ad. It's closed by default and can be closed again any time.
- **What applicants see:** the role, place, experience and what you're looking for, with an optional
  line of your own. They give name, email, a profile link, and optionally current role, city and a
  note, and tick a consent box.
- **Scored like everyone else.** Each application is scored against the role's saved brief by the same
  AI (instructions hidden in an application are ignored) and lands in **Found**, marked **Applied**,
  with the note in the summary.
- **Guard rails.** One application per email per role, 200 per role at most, a hidden field that catches
  simple bots, and the page says nothing about your targets, notes or other candidates.
- **Reaching it from the internet.** If n8n runs on your own computer, a free Cloudflare tunnel gives it a
  public address; [`docs/PUBLIC-ACCESS.md`](../docs/PUBLIC-ACCESS.md) shows how to expose only the apply
  and open-tracking pages.
