# v1.2: A team workspace

_AI Recruiting Sourcing Pipeline · built on [n8n](https://n8n.io)_

## Team logins

- Everyone signs in with their own email and password; the shared access code is gone.
- **Roles.** Admins manage the team, the sending settings and the follow-up emails. Recruiters search,
  review, vote and approve outreach.
- **First run.** The first visitor creates the admin account. If an access code was set before, it's
  asked once, to prove the workspace is yours.
- **Adding people.** Admins add teammates and get a one-time password to share privately. The
  teammate picks their own password on first sign-in. Admins can also reset passwords, change roles
  and turn accounts off. A turned-off account is signed out at once. The team always keeps at least
  one active admin.
- **Security.** Passwords are salted and hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256 (4,000 rounds) inside n8n, and
  never leave it. Sessions last 7 days and end on sign-out. n8n's sandboxed pages can't keep storage,
  so reloading the page asks you to sign in again.
- **Activity log.** Records who created the workspace, signed in, started a search, approved people,
  edited follow-ups, changed settings, or added or changed a teammate.

Data Tables: `app_users`, `app_sessions`, `app_activity` (new), plus `app_settings`.

## Home

- This week: searches, people found, strong matches, people in follow-up, replies and reply rate.
- **Needs your attention**: replies, shortlists with strong matches nobody has reviewed yet, and
  bounced emails.
- **Best sources**: found, strong, contacted and replied, per source.
- Team activity, and a getting-started checklist that ticks itself off: first search, first vote,
  first approval, own follow-up wording, signature, first teammate.

## Settings

- **Your account**: name, role, change password.
- **Team** (admins): add, change role, reset password, turn on or off.
- **Sending** (admins): signature, sending window, daily limit and weekdays-only. The follow-up
  runner reads these on every run.
- **Connected services**: Serper, OpenAI, GitHub, Apollo and Gmail, with when each last produced
  results.

## Upgrading

The first time you open the app after upgrading, choose **Set up your workspace**. Enter your name,
email and a password, plus your current access code.
