# v1.3: The Board gets smarter

_AI Recruiting Sourcing Pipeline · built on [n8n](https://n8n.io)_

Everything here runs inside your n8n. Nothing is emailed unless a person presses a button.

## Replies

- **Read and draft.** On a Replied card, *Read and draft a reply* opens the candidate's latest message
  from the Gmail thread (quoted history stripped), tags it *Keen*, *Has a question*, *Not now* or
  *Not interested*, and drafts a short answer.
- **Free slots.** With Google Calendar connected, the draft offers three free 45-minute slots on the
  next weekdays (10:00–17:00 IST). Tap a slot to add it to the reply.
- **Send from Gmail.** The edited reply goes only to that candidate, in the same thread, and is logged
  in the card's history.
- **Not now.** Parks the person for 90 days; they come back on the board by themselves.

To turn on slots: add a *Google Calendar OAuth2* credential in n8n, open the **App FreeBusy** node,
select it, and enable the node. While it's off, drafts ask the candidate for times instead.

## Interview kit

Interview, Replied and Hired cards get *Interview questions*: five behavioural or work-sample
questions aimed at the role's must-haves and at what the profile doesn't yet show, each with what a
strong answer sounds like, plus a weighted scorecard. Protected attributes are never asked about.

## On the board

- **Pulse.** Days open, this role's reply rate next to the team's, the channel bringing the most good
  people, and when replies usually come in (once there are enough of them).
- **Forecast.** "Approve the 6 shortlisted with emails and expect about 1 reply in 7 days", with a
  one-click *Approve 6*. People emailed recently for another role are left out.
- **What if.** In the brief column: how many found people show every must-have, and how many more you
  would have if each must-have were a nice-to-have.
- **Find more like these.** Searches again using the titles and companies of the people you
  shortlisted.
- **Warm intros.** Each person adds the companies they've worked at (Settings → Account). Cards at
  those companies say who worked there, so you can ask for an intro.
- **Open to work** is flagged when the profile says so.
- **Who else is here.** "Priya on this board now" in the header.
- **The `/` command bar.** Jump to a role, a person or an action from the keyboard.

## Sending

- **No double emails.** Nobody is emailed twice within 90 days across all roles (change it in
  Settings → Sending).
- **Open tracking (optional, off by default).** Adds a 1×1 image to each email and shows "Opened 2×"
  on cards. It needs the public https address of your n8n, and it can hurt deliverability, so leave it
  off unless you need it.

Data Tables: `app_opens` (new). New webhook: `sourcing-open` (the tracking image).
