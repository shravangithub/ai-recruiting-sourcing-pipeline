# v1.4: More places to find people, all free

_AI Recruiting Sourcing Pipeline · built on [n8n](https://n8n.io)_

## New channels

- **Research papers.** Industry researchers from [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org): people at companies in
  your search country who published on the role's topic in the last four years, with their papers and
  citations. Good for AI/ML, R&D and applied-science roles. Free: about 100 searches a day without a key
  (each sourcing run uses one); a free OpenAlex key raises that tenfold.
- **Speakers & experts.** Conference speaker profiles (Sessionize), Google Developer Experts, Microsoft
  MVPs and AWS Heroes, by topic and country. Uses your existing Google search.

## New ways to search

- **Veterans only.** A switch in the brief (also picked up from words like "ex-servicemen" or "armed
  forces"). Searches LinkedIn for people from the Army, Navy and Air Force, by role and by rank, and
  scores what their service shows: rank, people led, operations, logistics, security, technical
  branches. Missing corporate titles are not held against them.
- **Target companies.** List up to five; each gets its own LinkedIn search, so no company is crowded
  out. The Board's new **Map** view shows who works where, by level (individual, senior, manager,
  head), with your target companies marked.
- **Past candidates first.** When you write a brief, the app looks through everyone from earlier
  searches and shows who fits, with why. Tick them and they are added to the new role when the search
  starts (or with "Add to this role" on an existing one), with a note in their history.

## Fixes

- **Google's hidden links.** Since mid-2026 Google wraps many result links as `google.com/goto?url=…`,
  and the search service passes some through. These are now rebuilt from the address Google shows, or
  tagged with the site the search was limited to, so LinkedIn people count as LinkedIn and duplicates
  aren't merged by mistake. The links still open the real profile in a browser. (We don't fetch them:
  Google answers scripts with a bot check.)
- **Company on every card.** The employer named in the search result is now kept; before, it only
  came from Apollo or GitHub, so most cards had none.
- Google's line under each result (place · title · company) now reaches the scorer.
