# v1.5: Run it without worrying

_AI Recruiting Sourcing Pipeline · built on [n8n](https://n8n.io)_

## Health

A new **Health** page (with a dot in the menu when something needs a look) checks, in plain words:

- **Searches finish**, or which ones are stuck and since when.
- **Follow-ups and first emails are running.** Both 15-minute jobs now leave a heartbeat; if one stops
  (n8n closed, computer asleep, workflow not published) you see it, and when it last ran.
- **Sending**: emails sent today against your daily limit, people in follow-up, your sending hours,
  and follow-ups that are late.
- **Bounces and failed emails** this week, with the reason.
- **Channels**: which ones found people and when; channels you keep picking that find nobody; three
  empty searches in a row (usually out of credits).
- **Setup**: missing email signature, only one admin.

Each problem comes with the usual fix.

## Weekly report

Every Monday at 9:17, admins get last week's numbers by email: searches, people found, strong matches,
first emails, replies, interviews and hires; a table by role, the best channels, what each teammate
did, and anything Health flagged. Preview it any time on the Health page, and switch it off there.
Nothing is sent in a week with no activity.
